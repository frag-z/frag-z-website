Game Modes

These are the game modes currently selected by the server entry point through data/server.ini.

Mode Config Value Goal
King Of The Hill KING_OF_THE_HILL Two teams fight over one hill. The owning team's hill timer counts down; rounds award points toward the match.
Capture The Flag CAPTURE_THE_FLAG Two teams steal the enemy flag, return it to base, and score captures.
Last One Standing LAST_ONE_STANDING Team rounds where the surviving team wins the round; enough rounds win a match, enough matches win the game.
Timed Free For All TIMED_FREE_FOR_ALL Timed deathmatch. Players frag for the match duration and the highest frag totals lead.
