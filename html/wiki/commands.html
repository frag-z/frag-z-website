Commands

Commands are sent as chat messages.

Command Where It Works Effect
/join Spectators in current modes. Join as a player on the default team.
/join red Team-capable modes. Join or switch to team A.
/join blue Team-capable modes. Join or switch to team B.
/ready King Of The Hill, Capture The Flag, and Last One Standing. Marks your player ready during ready-up.
/unready King Of The Hill, Capture The Flag, and Last One Standing. Clears your ready state.
/username NAME All current modes. Sets your displayed username.
/initials ABC All current modes. Sets your short displayed initials.
